Rule engines, live

Three of the engines from my schema mapping and reconciliation pipeline, working on specific cases. Change the data or the rules and the result updates as you type.

Everything here runs in your browser on made-up data. It is a simplified version written for this page; the full pipeline is Python and private because it handles sensitive data.

What is each column?

Every bank names and formats its export differently. The engine reads the column name and the values, scores its confidence, and only applies a mapping by itself when it is sure. Anything below the threshold goes to a person.

Why does the balance break?

When a running balance stops adding up, the engine works backwards. It tests each hypothesis against the exact gap, proposes a correction, then replays the statement to check the correction closes it.

Which lines belong together?

A bank statement against a cash book. Rules claim lines in ranked order, and a higher rule overrules a lower one. Switch a rule off or change the date tolerance to see what each rule contributes.
